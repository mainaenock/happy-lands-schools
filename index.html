Our story

Growing with families since 1993.

Happyland began with a handful of pre-schoolers in a rented Nairobi house. A primary section followed in 1999. Today, four campuses carry that same Christian foundation into classrooms, clubs and school life.

We want every child to feel known, be challenged in their learning and have the chance to discover what they love.
